# TipMUSD demo — voiceover script

Voice: Microsoft Andrew (en-US-AndrewNeural, edge-tts), normal speed. Recorded on Mezo Testnet with real transactions.

## intro
This is TipMUSD, the social tipping layer for Mezo. Anyone gets one link and one QR code, and supporters tip them in MUSD or MEZO, straight from wallet to wallet.

## how
Creators share their link anywhere. Supporters pick an amount and send it from their own wallet. TipMUSD never holds the money, and it counts a tip only after checking it on Mezo.

## start
Let's set up a creator. Maya connects with Mezo Passport, so Bitcoin wallets like Unisat, OKX and Xverse work, as well as Ethereum wallets. Signing in is a free signature, not a transaction.

## profile
She picks her link, adds her name and a short bio, and keeps the suggested amounts.

## live
Her page is live, with a link and a QR code she can put anywhere: on X, in her videos, or on a slide at a meetup.

## tip_page
Now a supporter, Leo, opens Maya's link from the QR code. He sees who he's supporting, suggested amounts, and a choice of MUSD or MEZO.

## pick
He picks five MUSD, and adds his name and a short thank-you note.

## connect
He connects his wallet, and the review screen shows exactly where the money goes: Maya's wallet address, the network fee in BTC, and his balances.

## review
Leo has Bitcoin, but no MUSD. TipMUSD doesn't send him away to find some. It offers to pay with his Bitcoin, right here, through Mezo.

## borrow_note
He has two Mezo options. He can swap a little BTC for MUSD on Mezo pools. Or he can borrow MUSD against his Bitcoin with Mezo Borrow. That means drawing a little more from a loan he already has, or opening a new one. A new Mezo loan starts at eighteen hundred MUSD, so here it's greyed out and says how much BTC it needs. Borrowing works the same way, but in this demo we'll swap.

## swap
He swaps a tiny amount of BTC for just over five MUSD. Two quick approvals, both on Mezo, and the MUSD is in his wallet.

## send
Now he sends the tip. The transfer goes straight from Leo's wallet to Maya's. TipMUSD waits for Mezo, then checks the transfer itself before it says anything.

## confirmed
Confirmed on Mezo. The receipt links to the transaction and says this tip was funded from Bitcoin via Mezo. That label only appears after the swap itself is checked on chain.

## mezo
Supporters can also tip in MEZO, Mezo's native token. Leo switches to MEZO, picks ten, and sends it the same way.

## dashboard
Back with Maya. Her dashboard shows what she's received, with MUSD and MEZO kept separate, her supporters, and how many tips were funded from Bitcoin. Every number here comes from confirmed transfers, never from what a browser claims.

## activity
Each tip shows its amount, its sender, the message, and a link to the transaction.

## explorer
Here it is on the Mezo explorer: a real MUSD transfer, from Leo's wallet straight to Maya's.

## share
Maya can download her QR code, print a card, or share her link to X, LinkedIn, Telegram and WhatsApp.

## explore
And the Explore page is the social layer: creators on Mezo, live public tips, and how many of them were paid with Bitcoin. Anonymous tips never appear here.

## outro
No custody, no new contracts: TipMUSD uses MUSD, MEZO, Mezo pools and Mezo Borrow, all listed in the footer. That's TipMUSD. One link, and anyone can support you on Mezo.
